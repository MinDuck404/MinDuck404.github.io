FeedLens

Fine-Tuned Vietnamese Whisper ASR & Subtitling App2024 - 2025

VN-SubMaker

Manual Vietnamese subtitling is slow, while general-purpose speech models miss Vietnamese tones. Fine-tuned Whisper-large-v3-turbo on 10 Vietnamese speech corpora (viVoice, VSV-1100, viet_vlsp, FLEURS, Bud500, and more) and built a Flask + Docker web app transcribing MP3, WAV, MP4, and recorded WebM into editable text, UTF-8 SRT, or FFmpeg-burned video. Added 5-second video chunking, Server-Sent Events (SSE) live progress, and Web Speech API live captions. Benchmarked via jiwer on 500 VLSP2020 files against openai/whisper-base, cutting average WER from 54.19% to 12.36% and CER from 31.64% to 7.66%.
